# Module 01 - Generative Art
### Daniel Landry

## Summary

For my generative art project, I have put on display a simulation of "Braitenberg's vehicles," which are simple machines that move differently in the face of a light source. Through a surpsingly small number of parameters, complex behavior emerges, and things get really interesting when the complex behaviors of different "vehicles" become dependent on each other, as was achieved in the simulation by making each vehicle a light source itself.
